\title{Cross-Repository Reference Test}
\author{Tianle Yang}
\date{\today}

\externalnotedocument[source]{template_book}

\begin{document}

\maketitle

Published entries retain the imported book, current number, and permanent tag:
\cref{source:thm:published-comparison} and
\cref{source:thm:untitled-published}. The same remains true after an unchanged
statement moves: \cref{source:prop:published-in-draft}.

Published structural labels behave the same way: \cref{source:chap:published},
\cref{source:sec:published}, and \cref{source:subsec:published}.

A draft entry has only its structural locator:
\cref{source:thm:untagged-draft}.

Draft structural labels omit their provisional tags:
\cref{source:chap:mixed-draft}, \cref{source:sec:mixed-draft}, and
\cref{source:subsec:mixed-draft}.

\end{document}
