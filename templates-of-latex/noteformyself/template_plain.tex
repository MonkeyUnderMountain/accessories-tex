\section[Universal properties]{Universal properties and comparison maps}
\label{sec:universal}

Ordinary section titles, optional theorem titles, and labels work without a

\begin{theorem}[Uniqueness]\label{thm:uniqueness}
  Two objects with the same universal property are uniquely isomorphic.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
  The two universal properties give maps in opposite directions. Uniqueness
  makes both composites the identity.
\end{proof}

See \cref{sec:universal,thm:uniqueness}.
